## Story spine (2 minute pitch / demo video)
1. **Hook (10 s):** a real moment, a real place, a real number.
2. **Problem (20 s):** who suffers, why current tools fail.
3. **Solution (20 s):** what we built, in one picture.
4. **Live demo (50 s):** one click path, scripted, rehearsed. Never improvise the demo.
5. **Proof (15 s):** baseline vs our model, one validated real event.
6. **Impact + next step (5 s):** who could use it tomorrow.

## Judge criteria checklist
- [ ] Influence: user + scale stated with a number
- [ ] Creativity: the one idea nobody else has, said out loud
- [ ] Validity: metric table + one known event reproduced
- [ ] Relevance: every challenge objective mapped to a feature (see CHALLENGE.md)
- [ ] Presentation: rehearsed 3 times, under time, English subtitles on video
